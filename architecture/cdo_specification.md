Commerce Decision Object (CDO)
Purpose

The Commerce Decision Object is the standardized decision representation exchanged between autonomous agents inside ACOS.

Instead of communicating raw messages, every agent produces a CDO.

The CDO enables:

Explainable decision making
Negotiation
Traceability
Experiment reproducibility
Decision auditing
