# Pricing Optimization Agent


## Purpose

The Pricing Optimization Agent autonomously determines optimal product pricing decisions based on market conditions, inventory status, and profitability.


## Inputs

- Product information
- Inventory level
- Demand condition
- Competitor pricing
- Market season


## Decision Output

The agent generates:

- Recommended action
- Price adjustment percentage
- Confidence score
- Expected business impact
- Risk assessment


## Future Integration

The agent will communicate with other agents using the Adaptive Commerce Negotiation Protocol (ACNP).
